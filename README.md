Feature Adoption & Onboarding FrictionEngagement depth directly shields accounts from competitor displacement.The Onboarding Gap: Customers using less than 50% of core features carry a 5.34% churn rate, accounting for 51.8% of all lost accounts (475 out of 916 total churns).Power User Lock-in: Crossing an 80% feature adoption threshold reduces account churn to near zero (0.25%).Takeaway: Retention is won during initial onboarding. Unassisted users who fail to find multi-feature workflows cancel early.3. Support Ticket Tipping Point: Disproving "Silent Churn"A common assumption in SaaS is that non-communicative accounts ("silent churn") are at highest risk. The data disproves this hypothesis.Silent Accounts (0 Tickets): Accounts with zero support interactions show nominal churn rates.The Tipping Point: Risk scales modestly up to 3 support tickets, but escalates exponentially once an account logs 4+ tickets.Critical Friction: Accounts submitting 6 or more support tickets exhibit extreme churn rates, signaling unresolved product friction or technical failure.4. Industry Revenue Paradox: Gross MRR vs. Revenue LeakageEvaluating retention strictly by user count masks financial impact. Mapping churn directly to Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) highlights where Customer Success (CS) interventions yield the highest dollar ROI.Top Gross MRR Driver: CyberSecurity generates the highest gross revenue at $4,346,064, while maintaining low revenue leakage ($42,513 lost MRR/month).The Revenue Leak: MarTech suffers the heaviest financial loss to churn, bleeding $67,748 in lost MRR monthly—despite producing the lowest overall gross MRR ($3,948,763).Second Highest Loss: EdTech loses $66,608 in lost MRR per month.Actionable Business Recommendations1. Implement Automated Seat-Activation CampaignsAction: Trigger automated email sequences and CS check-ins when an account remains under 30% seat utilization 14 days after purchase.Target Impact: Reducing "shelfware" accounts directly targets the highest relative churn risk group (7.94%).2. Redesign Onboarding around "Core Feature Milestones"Action: Restructure initial customer onboarding to guide accounts toward activating at least 3 core features within their first 30 days.Target Impact: Moving low-adoption users (<50%) into the medium tier (>50%) protects the cohort responsible for over half of total churn volume.3. Deploy High-Priority Support Escalation TriggersAction: Configure CRM alerts to automatically flag accounts that log their 4th support ticket within a 60-day window for immediate Customer Success intervention.Target Impact: Intercepts unhappy accounts before technical friction turns into cancellation.4. Realign Customer Success Resources by Revenue at RiskAction: Reallocate CS coverage from CyberSecurity accounts to dedicated onboarding specialists for MarTech and EdTech accounts.Target Impact: Recaptures up to $134,356 in monthly recurring revenue leaking from the two most vulnerable verticals.
